# NC GS Chapter 93A (learning excerpt)

## G.S. 93A-1. License required

It is unlawful for any person, partnership, association, or corporation in this State to act as a real estate broker without first obtaining a license issued by the North Carolina Real Estate Commission. Acting as a broker includes listing, offering, attempting, or agreeing to list real estate for sale; selling, offering, attempting, or agreeing to sell; buying or offering to buy; negotiating the purchase, sale, or exchange of real estate; or leasing or renting real estate for others, for a fee, commission, or other valuable consideration.

## G.S. 93A-2. Definitions (study notes)

A real estate broker is a person who, for compensation, assists others in the sale, purchase, lease, or exchange of real estate. A provisional broker is a licensee who must be under the supervision of a broker-in-charge until education and experience requirements are met. The Commission may also license real estate firms.

## G.S. 93A-6. Disciplinary authority (summary of public statute)

The Commission may reprimand, suspend, or revoke a license for conduct including misrepresentation, false promises, undisclosed dual compensation, failure to account for trust money, unworthiness or incompetence, and violating Commission rules. Trust monies belonging to others must be deposited and maintained as required by law and rule.
